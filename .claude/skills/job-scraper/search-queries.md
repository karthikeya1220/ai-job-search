## Location Filters

### Hyderabad (Primary)
- **Ideal:** Hyderabad, Telangana (within city)
- **Acceptable:** Remote / Hybrid with occasional office in Hyderabad
- **Borderline:** Secunderabad,HITEC City (greater Hyderabad area)
- **Too far:** Outside Telangana state (unless fully remote)

### Bengaluru (Secondary)
- **Ideal:** Bengaluru, Karnataka (within city)
- **Acceptable:** Remote / Hybrid with occasional office in Bengaluru
- **Borderline:** Electronic City, Whitefield (greater Bengaluru area)
- **Too far:** Outside Karnataka state (unless fully remote)

- Always search in English (CV language)
- Include both Hyderabad and Bengaluru in all queries
- Prioritize SDE/Backend/Full-Stack roles over niche specializations
- Include "entry level" or "junior" filters where appropriate
- Monitor for AI/ML integration roles as secondary opportunities
