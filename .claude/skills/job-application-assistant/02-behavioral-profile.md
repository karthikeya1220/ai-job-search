# Behavioral Profile

## Overview
Darshan Karthikeya's behavioral profile identifies them as an **Ownership-Driven Builder** pattern. They thrive in fast-paced environments where they can take end-to-end responsibility for features, from architecture through production deployment, while collaborating closely with cross-functional teams.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Achievement | High | Drives to deliver measurable results (sub-200ms latency, 90%+ test coverage, 65% reduction in manual effort) |
| Collaboration | High | Works effectively with PMs, designers, QA, and non-technical stakeholders |
| Growth | High | Actively mentors junior developers and continuously learns new technologies |
| Quality | High | Maintains high code coverage standards and follows TDD practices |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **End-to-End Ownership:** Takes features from whiteboard design through production deployment, maintaining accountability throughout
- **Technical Excellence:** Consistently achieves high test coverage (85-95%) and optimized performance metrics
- **Team Collaboration:** Works effectively in Agile/Scrum environments with cross-functional teams

## How You Work Best
- Fast-paced, high-impact environments with diverse teams
- Clear ownership of features and modules
- Collaborative Agile/Scrum workflows with regular delivery cycles
- Working with modern tech stacks (Node.js, React, PostgreSQL)

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
